# Zapier

Use a Zapier account that belongs to an MCP-enabled account or workspace.
Zapier MCP is available on every Zapier plan. If Zapier MCP is disabled, ask
your Zapier account manager to enable it for the workspace. If workspace
access is restricted, ensure the account is a workspace member. You sign in
to Zapier in your browser when first asked after you [connect your
credentials](speakeasy.md#connect-speakeasy-credentials).

An app connection owned by that account allows Zapier to make its actions
available immediately, but you do not need one to establish the MCP
connection.
